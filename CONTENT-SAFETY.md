# Content governance

DeadNET may be used in stressful and life-threatening situations. Content quality is a safety requirement.

Every future knowledge article should include:
- Topic and intended audience
- Author/reviewer and qualifications where relevant
- Primary source(s) and jurisdiction
- Publication/review date
- Clear warnings and contraindications
- Steps that require professional training
- Conditions where the user should stop and seek emergency help
- Version history

Do not publish unverified folk remedies, improvised drug dosing, unsafe chemical disinfection recipes, dangerous electrical work, structural engineering shortcuts or instructions that facilitate violence. Medical content must be reviewed by qualified clinicians and aligned with recognized first-aid guidance.
